About the event

SEU Tech Innovation Day 2026

The SEU Tech Innovation Day 2026 is a student-focused event designed to introduce practical ideas in web development, programming, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies.

The event encourages students to learn through demonstrations, short talks, guided activities, and peer interaction. Participants can explore different technology tracks and identify areas for future study and project work.

Official university information is available at Southeast University.

Learning and participation

Event Goals and Topic Plan

Event Goals

  • Introduce students to current technology fields.
  • Encourage practical learning and teamwork.
  • Connect classroom knowledge with real web projects.
  • Build confidence for future technical events.
